PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Find & Help ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

By using Find & Help, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, location, and password when registering
  • Case Reports: Information about missing persons, including descriptions, photos, last seen details, and circumstances
  • Sightings: Information you submit about potential sightings, including location, time, description, and contact preferences
  • Contact Information: Details you provide so we or relevant parties can follow up

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage data (pages visited, time spent, actions taken)
  • Device and browser information
  • IP address and general location data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Facilitate the reporting and management of missing persons cases
  • Share relevant case information with authorized users and the community as intended
  • Verify and process sightings and reports
  • Communicate with you about cases, updates, or platform improvements
  • Maintain platform security and prevent misuse
  • Improve our services and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We understand the sensitive nature of missing persons cases. We may share information as follows:

  • Case Visibility: Case details and sightings may be visible to other users as part of platform functionality
  • Service Providers: With trusted third parties who help operate our platform, under confidentiality obligations
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect safety and rights
  • Safety: If necessary to protect the safety of individuals or the public
  • Consent: With your explicit consent for other purposes

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but strive to protect your data to the best of our ability.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide services, fulfill purposes outlined in this policy, or comply with legal obligations. Case information may be retained to support ongoing search efforts or historical records as appropriate.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access & Update: You can review and update your account information
  • Opt-Out: You may opt out of non-essential communications
  • Data Deletion: You may request deletion of your account or certain information, subject to legal and operational constraints
  • Cookies: You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings

8. Sensitive Information

Missing persons cases involve sensitive personal information. We ask users to share only information that is accurate, relevant, and necessary. We do not knowingly solicit unnecessary private details and take precautions to handle sensitive data responsibly.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will take steps to delete the information.

10. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the platform's contact channels.
