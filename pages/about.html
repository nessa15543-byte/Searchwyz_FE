ABOUT FIND & HELP

Bringing communities together to help find missing persons

Find & Help is a platform designed to connect communities, families, and concerned citizens in the search for missing persons. Every piece of information shared can help bring someone home safely.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a centralized, accessible platform where missing persons cases can be reported, tracked, and shared with the community to support search efforts and reunite families.

How We Help

  • Provide a simple way to report missing persons cases
  • Allow community members to submit verified sightings
  • Organize case information in one accessible location
  • Help prioritize and coordinate search efforts

Community-Driven

We believe that timely information saves lives. By empowering communities to share credible information, we help create a network of support for families searching for their loved ones.

Our Values

  • Compassion: We prioritize the dignity and wellbeing of families
  • Accuracy: We emphasize verified information
  • Accessibility: Simple tools for everyone
  • Privacy: Respecting sensitive information

Get Involved

If you have information about any missing person, please submit a sighting or report a case. Every detail, no matter how small, could make a difference.
