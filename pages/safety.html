SAFETY GUIDELINES

Safety Guidelines

Your safety and the safety of others is our top priority. Please follow these guidelines when using Find & Help.

Reporting Safely

  • Only report accurate, verifiable information. Do not speculate or spread unconfirmed rumors.
  • When creating a case, be careful not to share sensitive personal details that could put someone at unnecessary risk.
  • Respect the privacy and dignity of the missing person and their family.

Submitting Sightings

  • Only submit sightings if you have firsthand, credible information.
  • Include specific, factual details (time, location, description) without assumptions.
  • If you believe someone is in immediate danger, call emergency services immediately. Do not wait to submit a report.

Emergency Situations

In case of emergency: Call your local emergency services (112, 911, or your local emergency number) immediately. Find & Help is not an emergency response service.

Staying Safe Online

  • Never share private contact details publicly beyond what is necessary.
  • Be cautious of scams or requests for money - legitimate search efforts will not ask for payment through unsolicited messages.
  • Report suspicious activity to platform administrators.
  • Protect your account credentials and log out on shared devices.

Respect & Sensitivity

  • Treat all cases with compassion and respect.
  • Avoid sharing graphic details or speculative theories that could harm families.
  • Follow all applicable laws and local regulations.
